## Which recorded failures were real?

The original 29 noncompensable failure entries were not 29 independent defects. The diagnostic audit found:

- **21 entries with concrete artifact evidence**, including duplicated name/disclosure gates, scaled modal communication problems, and one document-overflow failure.
- **Eight entries requiring qualification:** three exact-hook-text data mismatches, two hidden-label flow false positives, and three authority judgments that did not establish false author claims.

The earlier broad descriptions of “incorrect scientific data” and false verification claims were not justified for those disputed entries. Supplied quantities and complete field text were retained. Genuine defects remain:

- One current-arm artifact retained genuine document overflow. Correcting an unrelated hidden label must not waive it.
